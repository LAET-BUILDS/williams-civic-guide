California Public Records Act (CPRA)

Official text: Government Code §7920.000 and following Unverified[1]. Start at §7920.000 on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov. The official site blocked automated downloads when we built this page, so the full text is not reproduced here; open the link in a normal browser.

The California Constitution says the people have "the right of access to information concerning the conduct of the people's business" (Art. I, §3(b)(1)) Verified[2]. Read Article I, Section 3.

Unofficial plain-language summary

Unofficial summary Not legal advice; the statute controls.

Topic Section What it says (summary) Label
Response time §7922.535 The agency must decide within 10 days whether it has disclosable records and tell you; it may extend by up to 14 days in unusual circumstances. Unverified[3]
Partial release §7922.525 Release the parts that are not exempt. Unverified[4]
Help with your request §7922.600 The agency should help you identify records and narrow a request. Unverified[4]
Court enforcement §7923.000, §7923.100 You can sue to enforce the Act; a successful requester can get court costs and attorney's fees. Unverified[1]

Every row is unverified: check each section on leginfo before you cite it in a request.
