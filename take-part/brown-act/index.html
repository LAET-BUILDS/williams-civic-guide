The Ralph M. Brown Act (open meetings law)

Official text: California Government Code, Title 5, Division 2, Part 1, Chapter 9, starting at §54950 on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov Unverified[1]. The official site blocked automated downloads when we built this page, so the full text is not reproduced here; open the link in a normal browser.

The California Constitution also guarantees access: "the meetings of public bodies and the writings of public officials and agencies shall be open to public scrutiny" (Art. I, §3(b)(1)) Verified[2]. Read Article I, Section 3.

Unofficial plain-language summary

Unofficial summary Not legal advice; the statute controls.

Topic Section What it says (summary) Label
Purpose §54950 Local agencies exist to do the people's business, and their actions and deliberations should be done openly. Unverified[1]
Agenda posting §54954.2 Regular meeting agendas must be posted at least 72 hours before the meeting, with a brief description of each item. Generally, no action on items not on the agenda. Unverified[1]
Public comment §54954.3 The public must have a chance to address the body on items within its jurisdiction. Reasonable time limits are allowed; criticism may not be prohibited; speakers using a translator get double time. Verified[3]
Special meetings §54956 Generally require 24 hours' notice. Unverified[1]
Closed sessions §54956.7-§54957.x Allowed only for listed topics (for example, some personnel, labor negotiations, litigation, real estate). Unverified[1]
Reporting closed-session actions §54957.1 Certain actions taken in closed session must be reported publicly. Unverified[1]
Enforcement §54960 Anyone can sue to stop or prevent violations. Unverified[1]
Cure or correct §54960.1 Before suing to void an action, send a written demand: within 90 days (30 days for certain agenda violations); the body has 30 days to respond; then 15 days to sue. Verified[4]

"Verified" above means we read that section's text on an unofficial copy (Justia); "Unverified" means it comes from a summary or general knowledge. Check every section on leginfo before relying on it.
