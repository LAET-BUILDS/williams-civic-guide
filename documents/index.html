Founding documents

The two constitutions are the top two layers of law that every Williams rule must fit under. Full texts are copied from official government sources; summaries are unofficial and in plain language.

Type at least 3 letters. Results link to the exact section.

    • Federal · Layer 1

      U.S. Constitution and all 27 amendments

      Full text, with links to every article, section and amendment, a search box, and unofficial summaries.

      Official source: National Archives transcriptions Verified[1][2][3]; also the Constitution Annotated Unverified[4]

    • State · Layer 2

      California Constitution

      Full text, one page per article, searchable, with unofficial summaries for key articles.

      Official source: California State Senate 2025-26 publication (as amended through Nov 5, 2024) Verified[5]; also leginfo Unverified[6]

    Guides to the Brown Act and the Public Records Act are under Take part.

    Good places to start

    • U.S. Constitution, Article VI (federal law is supreme) and the Fourteenth Amendment (due process, equal protection).
    • California Constitution, Article I (rights) and Article XI, §7 (city ordinances may not conflict with general laws).

    How to read these pages

    • Use the Contents list, the search box, or your browser's find (Ctrl+F / "Find on page").
    • Every article and section has its own link, so you can share an exact spot.
    • The California text was extracted automatically from the official PDF. If anything looks wrong, the official PDF controls; please report it.
    • For a plain-language guide to what these texts mean for Williams, see Constitutions.
